LEGAL INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Tech World handles information when you visit our website or contact us about a project.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use our contact form, we may collect your name, email address, project type, budget range, message, and any attachment you choose to provide.

We collect limited analytics about visits to publicly accessible pages of our website using a daily pseudonymous identifier. Raw IP addresses are not stored in our analytics records. Analytics records are deleted according to our configured data retention schedule. Although the identifier is pseudonymous, it may still be considered personal data under applicable privacy laws.

Our hosting provider and website infrastructure may also process technical information required to deliver and protect the website.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through the contact form may be used to:

  • Review and respond to your project enquiry.
  • Send an enquiry notification to our configured email inbox.
  • Maintain and manage enquiry records through authorized administrative access.
  • Protect and maintain the website.

3. Contact Form Records and Attachments

Contact submissions are stored in our application database. If you upload an attachment, it may be stored with the application’s uploaded files.

Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, identity documents, or other sensitive information through the contact form.

4. Email Notifications

Contact enquiries are used to generate email notifications through the configured email service. Email providers and infrastructure providers may process information as needed to deliver those messages.

Email delivery can fail. A submission may still be saved even when its notification email cannot be delivered.

5. Data Retention

We retain enquiry records and attachments only for as long as reasonably needed for communication, project management, legitimate operational needs, and applicable legal obligations. A precise retention schedule should be established and followed by the website operator.

6. Sharing and Service Providers

We do not intend to sell contact-form information. Information may be processed by service providers involved in hosting, database operation, email delivery, security, and maintenance. It may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

7. Security

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect website data. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your Requests

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Contact the website operator using the email address displayed on our Contact page to make a request.

9. Children

This website is intended for general business enquiries, not for collecting personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when website features, operational practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, please use the contact information available on our Contact page.
